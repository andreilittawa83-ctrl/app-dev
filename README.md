# Suits

![Rating badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Rating-10%2F10-brightgreen)

## Why I Like It
**Clever writing** and *unforgettable characters* make Suits worth watching. It follows Mike Ross, a brilliant college dropout who lands a job at a top New York law firm without a law degree. :briefcase:

### Top Reasons
- Sharp dialogue and clever plot twists
- Great chemistry between Harvey and Mike
- Strong character development, especially Donna, Louis, and Jessica
